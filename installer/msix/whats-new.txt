See your screen as it will be: each zone once, numbered and coloured, with its windows in it. Drag windows onto each other to swap them and drag the lines between zones to resize them, then Arrange my screen (with undo). The same on a workspace's picture, saved at once. Every layout in view, in two rows. Click a workspace to see how it will open, then Open. Arrange tidies your windows without making or changing a workspace; Save as workspace keeps what is on screen. Larger text and buttons, plainer words, and quicker. Zones are numbered 1, 2, 3 by place; a workspace's page lists its apps zone by zone. Tabs get a strip of their own, each one easy to drag out. Opening a workspace clears its screens of other windows. Since 1.0.0: a new window, dark by default, your apps by their own icons, no more pop-ups. Zones make room for a window that needs more, windows meet edge to edge, and the side menu at the screen's edge has Arrange, layouts and workspaces.

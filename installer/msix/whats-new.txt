Click a workspace to see how it will open, then Open. Larger text and buttons, plainer words, and quicker. Zones are numbered 1, 2, 3 by place; a workspace's page shows its apps as rows of zone dots. Tabs get a strip of their own, each one easy to drag out. Opening a workspace clears its screens of other windows. Since 1.0.0: a new window, dark by default. See your screen as it will be before anything moves: Automatic lays it out for what you are doing, or pick one of ten layouts, and drag a window onto another to swap them; then Arrange (with undo). Your workspaces beside it, your apps by their own icons, no more pop-ups. Zones make room for a window that needs more, windows meet edge to edge, and the side menu at the screen's edge has Arrange, layouts and workspaces.

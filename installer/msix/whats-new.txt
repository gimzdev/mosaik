See your screen as it will be: each zone once, numbered and coloured, with its windows in it. Drag a window into a zone to add it there, or onto another to swap them or bring it to the front, and drag the lines between zones to resize them, then Move windows (with undo): they stay so, each in its colour. Fewer zones than windows: the rest stack in the zones instead of being minimised. The same on a workspace's picture, saved at once, and a layout clicked there keeps all its apps. Add any app to a workspace, open or not. Every layout in view, in two rows. Click a workspace to see how it will open, then Open. Save as workspace keeps what is on screen. Larger text and buttons, plainer words, and quicker. Tabs get a strip of their own, each one easy to drag out. Opening a workspace clears its screens of other windows. Since 1.0.0: a new window, dark by default, your apps by their own icons, no more pop-ups. Zones make room for a window that needs more, windows meet edge to edge, and the side menu at the screen's edge has Move windows, layouts and workspaces.
